KBLeagues ("we," "our," or "us") built the CourtPulse NBA mobile application for Android. This Privacy Policy explains how CourtPulse NBA accesses, collects, uses, handles, and shares user data.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, track, or sell your personal browsing activity, usage analytics, location data, or device identifiers.

To allow you to sign in to CourtPulse NBA, the app uses Google Sign-In. When you authenticate using your Google account, we receive only the basic account information you authorize Google to share:

2. How We Use Your Information

The authentication information provided through Google Sign-In is used strictly for core app functionality:

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with advertisers, data brokers, or marketing networks.

The only third party that processes or receives data in connection with CourtPulse NBA is Google LLC, strictly for authentication and core Android platform services:

  • Google Sign-In (authentication and account access) — Google Privacy Policy

All authentication data is transmitted securely over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) using Google's OAuth 2.0 protocol. We take commercially reasonable steps to protect your information, though no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure.

5. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain your basic Google Sign-In information only for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide you access to CourtPulse NBA.

You may revoke CourtPulse NBA's access to your Google account at any time via your Google Account Permissions. To request permanent deletion of your account and any associated data stored by us, email kbleaguesdev@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request - CourtPulse NBA". We will process your request within 30 days.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us at kbleaguesdev@gmail.com so we can promptly delete it.
